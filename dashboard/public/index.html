Quick guide

Edit mappings under Servers, the template under Roles & Channels, or ticket buttons and panels under Tickets & Panels. Saving applies changes in place through live sync. Existing resources and message history are preserved; nothing is recreated or cleaned up.

Security notes

Passwords are stored as scrypt hashes (never plain text). Login is rate-limited (8 tries / 10 min), sessions are HttpOnly + SameSite=Lax cookies expiring after 12h, and all mutations require a CSRF token. Serve this dashboard behind HTTPS and keep it off the public internet if possible.
